Refinancing Tips to Maximize Savings

When Does Refinancing Make Sense?

Generally, refinancing makes sense when the new rate is at least 0.75-1% lower than your current rate. Use this calculator to find your exact breakeven point.

Reduce Your Loan Term

If you're able to afford a higher monthly payment, refinancing to a 15-year mortgage instead of 30 can save you hundreds of thousands in interest.

Lock in a Fixed Rate

If you have an adjustable rate mortgage (ARM), refinancing to a fixed rate protects you from future rate increases.
