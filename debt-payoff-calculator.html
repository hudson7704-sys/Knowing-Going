Ways to Pay Off Debt Faster

1. Pay More Than the Minimum

Even an extra $50-100 per month can cut years off your payoff timeline and save thousands in interest.

2. Consolidate Your Debt

If you have multiple debts, consolidating them into a single loan with a lower interest rate can save you money and simplify your payments.

3. Negotiate a Lower Interest Rate

Contact your creditors and ask if they'll lower your rate. A 2-3% reduction can save you hundreds or thousands.

4. Use the Snowball or Avalanche Method

Snowball: Pay off smallest debts first for quick wins. Avalanche: Pay off highest-rate debts first to save the most money.
